💧 The Water Jug Puzzle

Measure exactly 4 liters using the two jugs.

🧠 Your Challenge

You have two empty jugs: a 5-liter jug and a 3-liter jug.

You have an unlimited supply of water. Fill, empty, and pour between the jugs however you like.

🎯 Goal: Get exactly 4 liters into the 5-liter jug.

🖱️ Drag a jug onto the other jug to pour water.

🧠 Solution

Step 1

Fill the 5-liter jug completely.

State: (5L, 0L)

Step 2

Pour the 5L jug into the 3L jug.

State: (2L, 3L)

Step 3

Empty the 3-liter jug.

State: (2L, 0L)

Step 4

Pour the 2 liters from the 5L jug into the 3L jug.

State: (0L, 2L)

Step 5

Fill the 5-liter jug again.

State: (5L, 2L)

Step 6

Pour from the 5L jug into the 3L jug until the 3L jug is full.

Exactly 1 liter leaves the 5L jug.

State: (4L, 3L)

🎉 You now have exactly 4 liters in the 5-liter jug!
