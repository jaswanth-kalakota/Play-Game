🧠 Complete Solution

Step 1 — First Weighing

Put three balls on the left and three balls on the right.

⚪ ⚪ ⚪   VS   ⚪ ⚪ ⚪

Leave two balls aside.

Case 1 — The Scale Balances

The heavier ball must be one of the two balls left aside.

Compare those two balls.

⚪   VS   ⚪

The heavier side identifies the heavier ball.

Case 2 — One Side Is Heavier

Suppose the left side is heavier. The heavy ball is among those three balls.

Compare two of those three balls.

⚪ ⚪   VS   ⚪ ⚪

If one side is heavier, the heavy ball is on that side. If they balance, the third ball is the heavy one.

🎯 Why It Works

The first weighing reduces the possibilities from 8 balls to at most 3.

The second weighing identifies the heavier ball among those remaining possibilities.
