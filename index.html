🪙 Blindfolded Coin Puzzle

Divide the coins into two groups containing the same number of Heads.

🧠 Your Challenge

You are blindfolded, so you cannot tell which side any coin is showing.

Your goal is to divide all 10 coins into Group A and Group B so that both groups contain the same number of Heads.

🧠 Why the Solution Works

In this 10-coin demonstration, there is exactly 1 Head and 9 Tails.

Step 1

Choose any one coin and put it in Group A.

Step 2

Put the remaining 9 coins in Group B.

Step 3

Flip the single coin in Group A.

Why?

If the selected coin was the Head, flipping it changes it into a Tail. Both groups then have 0 Heads.

If the selected coin was a Tail, flipping it changes it into a Head. The original Head is in Group B. Therefore both groups have 1 Head.

🌎 Original 100-Coin Puzzle

The original puzzle contains 100 coins: 10 Heads and 90 Tails.

Choose any 10 coins and put them in Group A. Put the remaining 90 coins in Group B.

Now flip every coin in Group A.

Suppose the 10 selected coins originally contained X Heads. After flipping them, Group A contains 10 − X Heads.

Since there were originally 10 Heads total, Group B also contains 10 − X Heads.

Therefore both groups contain the same number of Heads.
